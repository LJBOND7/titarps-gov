Mission Statement

and company profile
Titanium Tarps LLC · Miami, Florida

Our mission

To protect damaged and exposed buildings with roof and enclosure systems that actually hold, so agencies, institutions, and the people they serve stay dry, safe, and operating while they recover and rebuild.

We exist because the blue tarp fails. A 90-day product on a 12-month problem costs the owner repeat mobilizations, interior damage, and lost use of the facility. We engineered a membrane, an attachment system, and a crew model that seal a building once and keep it sealed until the permanent roof is on.

What we stand for

  • The product has to perform. When the storm hits, the protection holds. Reliability is the entire promise.
  • Protect people when they are most vulnerable. Our customers call after a disaster. We treat that moment with the seriousness it deserves.
  • Engineer, test, document. Every claim is backed by a test report or a photograph, available to the buyer.
  • Deal straight. Clear scopes, honest schedules, and a named point of contact who answers the phone.
  • Make it here. Elastowrap is a domestic end product made to our own formulation, so the material is there when the forecast turns.

Who we are

Titanium Tarps LLC was formed in 2023 as the manufacturing and installation company of the Structural Wrap group, founded by Larry Bond, Chris Mouriz, and Spiro Naos. Titanium Tarps LLC and affiliate Structural Wrap LLC operate under common ownership with the same principals, crews, and equipment. The group has protected storm-damaged property since Hurricane Irma in 2017 and has responded to every major Gulf and Atlantic hurricane since. Titanium Tarps holds the federal registration (UEI VZ6EGCGK8416, CAGE 9SZZ2). Day-to-day operations are led by Marcelo Romero, Director of Operations.

How to engage us

  • Market research and RFIs: request a requirement-specific capability statement.
