01 / Core capabilities

Four defined scopes. One coordinated system.

Material, attachment, and installation are engineered together and delivered by the same team. Every scope is documented before, during, and after for the procurement and insurance file.

01

Temporary roof protection

Tensioned Elastowrap membrane coverage for roofs opened by hurricane, hail, wind, or fire, and for roofs left exposed while permanent roofing is designed, procured, or delayed. Mechanically attached to the structure with zero penetrations, rated for up to 24 months of exposure.

Emergency stabilization · Reroof delays · Historic and tile roofs · Campus and portfolio scale

02

Building and scaffold enclosure

Weather enclosure for scaffolded structures, open facades, and floor plates awaiting permanent exterior systems. Keeps interior trades working through weather and keeps debris contained, integrated with existing netting and screening.

Scaffold wrap · Open-floor enclosure · Delayed glazing · Stadium and high-rise scale

03

Interior work-area containment

Membrane partitions and suspended barriers that separate renovation, abatement, or repair areas from occupied space. Attachment and loads are evaluated per project. Class A fire-rated material suits occupied and secured facilities.

Atriums · Dust and debris barriers · Interior separation · Occupied facilities

04

Elastowrap membrane systems and supply

Elastowrap 12 mil stretch roof film, TitanBond seam tape, TitanGrip waterproof tape, TitanVent self-tapping vents, and TitanClip attachment hardware. Manufactured in our own plant, sold by the roll or by the truckload, with installation planning and training available.

Plastic sheeting supply (PSC 9330) · Roll sizes 20x100, 32x90, 32x70, 40x60 · Distributor and bulk pricing

02 / Differentiators

Why contracting officers and primes call us.

  • 150+ mph

    Wind tested at the International Hurricane Center, Florida International University. Report available on request.

  • Class A

    ASTM E84 / UL 723 fire rated, zero flame spread. Suitable for occupied and secured sites.

  • 12 mil

    Heaviest stretch roof film in its class, LDPE/LLDPE blend, 50% more tear resistant than comparable films.

  • 24 mo

    UV-rated service life. Installed once, dry until the permanent roof is finished.

  • 0

    Penetrations. Tensioned and clipped to the structure, no nails or screws through the roof deck, no sandbags.

We manufacture the film

Titanium Tarps is not a reseller. Elastowrap runs on our own production line, so material is consistent, Class A rated, and never on back order when the forecast turns.

We mobilize as one unit

Crews, equipment, and film stage from Miami while a storm is still tracking and deploy across the continental U.S., Gulf Coast, Puerto Rico, and the Caribbean. Installers and material travel together.

We document everything

Drone photography before, during, and after every installation, plus product specifications and test reports on request, so the procurement, FEMA Public Assistance, and insurance files are complete.

06 / Mission

Our mission

To protect damaged and exposed buildings with roof and enclosure systems that actually hold, so agencies, institutions, and the people they serve stay dry, safe, and operating while they recover and rebuild.

We exist because the blue tarp fails. A 90-day product on a 12-month problem costs agencies repeat mobilizations, interior damage, and lost use of the facility. We engineered a membrane, an attachment system, and a crew model that seal a building once and keep it sealed until the permanent roof is on.

What we stand for

  • The product has to perform. When the storm hits, the protection holds. Reliability is the entire promise.
  • Protect people when they are most vulnerable. Our customers call after a disaster. We treat that moment with the seriousness it deserves.
  • Engineer, test, document. Every claim we make is backed by a test report or a photograph, available to the buyer.
  • Deal straight. Clear scopes, honest schedules, and a named point of contact who answers the phone.
  • Build American supply. We manufacture our own film so the material is there when the forecast turns.
