Company overview

Titanium Tarps LLC manufactures the Elastowrap stretch roof membrane and installs it with its own crews on government, institutional, and industrial buildings left exposed by storm or fire damage, delayed reroofs, and active construction. Based in Miami, Florida, we mobilize across the United States, Puerto Rico, and the Caribbean. One scope, one crew, sealed and documented until the permanent roof is on.

Core competencies

Temporary roof protection

Tensioned membrane over storm-, hail-, or fire-damaged roofs and roofs awaiting replacement. Zero penetrations, up to 24 months.

Building and scaffold enclosure

Weather enclosure of scaffolded structures, open facades, and floor plates so interior trades keep working.

Interior work-area containment

Membrane partitions and suspended barriers separating renovation or abatement areas from occupied space.

Membrane systems and supply

Elastowrap 12 mil film, TitanBond and TitanGrip tapes, TitanVent vents, TitanClip hardware. By the roll or truckload.

Mission Statement

and company profile
Titanium Tarps LLC · Miami, Florida

Our mission

To protect damaged and exposed buildings with roof and enclosure systems that actually hold, so agencies, institutions, and the people they serve stay dry, safe, and operating while they recover and rebuild.

We exist because the blue tarp fails. A 90-day product on a 12-month problem costs the owner repeat mobilizations, interior damage, and lost use of the facility. We engineered a membrane, an attachment system, and a crew model that seal a building once and keep it sealed until the permanent roof is on.

What we stand for

  • The product has to perform. When the storm hits, the protection holds. Reliability is the entire promise.
  • Protect people when they are most vulnerable. Our customers call after a disaster. We treat that moment with the seriousness it deserves.
  • Engineer, test, document. Every claim is backed by a test report or a photograph, available to the buyer.
  • Deal straight. Clear scopes, honest schedules, and a named point of contact who answers the phone.
  • Build American supply. We manufacture our own film so the material is there when the forecast turns.

Who we are

Titanium Tarps LLC was formed in 2023 as the manufacturing and installation company of the Structural Wrap group, founded by Larry Bond, Chris Mouriz, and Spiro Naos. The group has protected storm-damaged property since Hurricane Irma in 2017 and has responded to every major Gulf and Atlantic hurricane since. Titanium Tarps holds the federal registration (UEI VZ6EGCGK8416, CAGE 9SZZ2) and is the contracting entity for government work. Day-to-day operations are led by Marcelo Romero, Director of Operations.

How to engage us

  • Market research and RFIs: request a requirement-specific capability statement.
  • Open-market RFQs and micro-purchases: quote within one business day on roof area, condition, duration, and access.
  • Prime contractors: subcontract installation or supply Elastowrap for temporary roofing and enclosure task orders.
  • State and local agencies: emergency standby and cooperative purchasing on request.
